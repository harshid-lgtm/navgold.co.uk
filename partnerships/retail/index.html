Retail Partnership Programme

We invest in your products by purchasing stock directly from you, then leverage our Amazon expertise to sell them profitably. A true partnership where we share both investment and success.

How Our Retail Partnership Works

A straightforward investment partnership where we buy your products and handle everything from there. It's a complete hands-off solution for manufacturers and brands.

  1. 1

    We Purchase Stock

    We invest our capital to purchase your products directly from you at wholesale prices. No upfront costs for you.

  2. 2

    You Ship to Us

    You deliver the purchased stock to our UK warehouse or designated location. We handle all logistics from this point.

  3. 3

    We Ship to Amazon

    Our team handles FBA shipments, inventory management, and ensures your products are live and optimised on Amazon.

  4. 4

    We Sell & Market

    We manage all sales, advertising, customer service, and optimisation to maximise revenue and profitability.

  5. 5

    Repeat Cycle

    As products sell successfully, we reorder more stock, creating an ongoing profitable partnership cycle.
