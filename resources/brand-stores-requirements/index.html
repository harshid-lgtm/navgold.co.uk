Best Practices

Create a consistent visual identity throughout your entire store
Use high-quality lifestyle images that reflect your brand personality
Organise products into logical categories for easy browsing
Include your brand story to build emotional connection with customers
Optimise for mobile - many customers browse stores on mobile devices
Use clear, descriptive navigation labels
Feature your best-selling or newest products prominently
Include customer reviews and ratings where appropriate
Use professional product photography consistently
Test your store on different devices before publishing

Brand Store Guidelines

  • • Only available to Brand Registered sellers on Amazon
  • • Store must have at least 3 ASINs to publish
  • • All images must be owned by you or properly licensed
  • • No promotional language, prices, or external links allowed
  • • Store content must comply with Amazon's content policies
  • • Mobile optimisation is required - test on multiple devices
  • • Store approval can take 72 hours to 7 business days
  • • Analytics are available after store publishes
