Required Documentation

Prepare the necessary documents for a smooth account verification process. We'll guide you through each requirement step by step.

Business Information

  • Business registration documents
  • Business licence (if applicable)
  • Professional business email
  • Business address verification
  • VAT registration (UK/EU sellers)

Identity Verification

  • Government-issued photo ID
  • Proof of address (utility bill)
  • Bank account verification
  • Phone number verification
  • Business owner identification

Tax & Financial

  • Tax identification number
  • Bank account for payments
  • Chargeable payment method
  • Tax interview completion
  • Financial statements (if required)
